Privacy Policy for RievorAI

Last updated: August 24, 2026

RievorAI is a personal memory organization application designed to help users save, organize, search, and retrieve information they choose to store. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use RievorAI.

1. Information We Process

Depending on how you use RievorAI, the app may process:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication data.
  • Information and memories that you voluntarily enter into the app.
  • Photos or images that you choose to upload or analyze.
  • Voice input when you choose to use voice-based features.
  • Information necessary to synchronize and manage your account and saved content.
  • Technical information necessary for the operation and security of the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information is processed only as necessary to provide RievorAI features, including:

  • Creating and managing your account.
  • Saving and synchronizing your memories and content.
  • Searching and retrieving information you have saved.
  • Providing optional artificial intelligence features.
  • Analyzing photos or other content when you explicitly request an AI-powered feature.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the service.

3. Artificial Intelligence Features

Some RievorAI features use artificial intelligence to help organize, analyze, or retrieve information.

When you explicitly use an AI-powered feature, the content required to perform that request may be securely transmitted to the service providers used to process the request. Only information necessary to provide the requested functionality is sent for processing.

4. Photos and User Content

RievorAI accesses photos or images only when you choose to provide them to the app. Images may be processed to provide features that you explicitly request, including AI-assisted organization or analysis.

RievorAI does not claim ownership of the personal content that you provide.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

RievorAI may use third-party infrastructure and service providers for authentication, cloud storage, database functionality, and artificial intelligence processing.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide the relevant functionality and are subject to their respective privacy and security obligations.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers only when necessary to operate RievorAI, provide a feature requested by you, maintain security, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. Account and Data Deletion

RievorAI provides users with the ability to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

When an account deletion request is completed, the associated account information and stored user content are deleted, subject to any limited retention required for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or technical backup processes.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

RievorAI is not specifically directed to children. Users should use the service in accordance with the minimum age requirements applicable in their country and the requirements of the distribution platform.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection to certain processing.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when RievorAI changes or when necessary to comply with applicable requirements. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
